# Splash logo audit

The old first-launch asset is `android-app/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/kharcha_logo_reference.png`: it has a white background, the `Kharcha` wordmark, and the `SPLIT · TRACK · SETTLE` tagline.

The intended second logo is `android-app/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/kharcha_logo_mobile.webp`: it has the cream background, the `Kharcha` wordmark, and the `Split Smart Live Smart` tagline. `MainActivity.createLogoSplash()` renders this asset as the retained app splash overlay.

The Android manifest's `LauncherActivity` still declares `android.support.customtabs.trusted.SPLASH_IMAGE_DRAWABLE` as `@drawable/kharcha_logo_reference`, which is the remaining old launch-logo source. The final fix should remove that old TWA splash image metadata while preserving the MainActivity overlay and its existing timing/layout.
